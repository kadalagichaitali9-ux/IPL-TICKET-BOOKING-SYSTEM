Before you arrive

Stadium Guidelines

A few quick reminders for a smooth and enjoyable matchday.

01

Ticket Verification

Keep your valid ticket ready for scanning at the entry gate. Each ticket admits one person only.

02

ID Proof Requirement

Carry a valid government-issued photo ID that matches the details associated with your ticket.

03

Prohibited Items

Outside food and drink, professional cameras, and hazardous items are not permitted inside the stadium.
